I See Your Essence

A Manifesto by Reiki Spark

You shared your light when you were young and tender. Maybe you loved something, and no one noticed. Or someone teased you for it. So you learned to be quiet, and you began to believe that nothing you do matters.

It does. You do.

I know, because I lived hidden and unseen for much of my life. Here is what I have learned: the sun does not need anyone to acknowledge it. The sun knows how great it is. I can learn a lot from the sun, and so can you.

I believe that when we are truly seen, we shine.

When we touch our Essence, there is a deep peace that feels eternal.

When you work with me, you learn to look inward and meet every part of yourself. Your judgments soften like frost on the first sunrise of spring. You step out of the cave and into the meadow, to be seen by the people you choose.

Right before you book, you may hesitate. That hesitation is you knowing you will be changed. Even one step toward your genius is invigorating.

The moment you say yes to yourself, your new life begins.
