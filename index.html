The Foundations

How the Work Unfolds

Awareness

Patterns Seeking Resolution

Every symptom, every stuck place, is information. What repeats in a family, an illness, a struggle, a silence, is often a pattern seeking resolution, not a flaw to fix. We look at what's been trying to be seen.

Belonging

Everyone Belongs in the Family

In every family system, everyone belongs, including those who were excluded, forgotten, or never spoken of. When someone is left out of the family soul, the system tries to restore balance, sometimes through someone in a later generation. Naming what was excluded restores order and belonging.

The Body

The Healing Movement

This work is somatic, not intellectual. You don't think your way to insight, you feel into your body, and let the body lead. The movements that arise are often small, unrehearsed, and exactly right.

Lineage

The Mother's Line & The Father's Line

What shows up in your life now often has roots in what moved through the maternal and paternal lines before you: unresolved grief, exclusion, early loss, unacknowledged fate. Seeing where you stand in these two lines is often where clarity begins.

Meet Lynetta

Lynetta Ann Avery

Family Constellations Facilitator  ·  Reiki Teacher  ·  Sound Bath Guide

For most of my life, no one really saw me. Not my family. Not the relationships I kept walking into, one after another, hoping this one would finally be the one who did.

I tried everything to be seen. Therapy. Religion. Twelve-step rooms. Sweat lodge. Every workshop, every training, every healer I could find. I did the work, all of it, and still came home to the same ache: invisible, exhausted, and starting to believe nothing would ever work.

Then something shifted. I saw my own life from above, like watching a movie instead of drowning inside it. And underneath my whole story, I found something I hadn't expected: it was never really about me. What I was carrying had been handed down, mother to daughter, long before I was born. Once I could see it clearly, I didn't have to carry it anymore.

That's the moment I understood what I'd actually found. Not a technique from a book, something true in the body, true across generations, true in the quiet space between people where nothing is being said out loud, but everything is being felt. Family Constellations holds all of it: the body's wisdom, the weight a family carries without knowing it's carrying it, the chance to finally set down what was never yours.

I use it now to help others do what I did: stop performing, stop chasing, stop trying to earn a kind of seeing that was never going to come from where they kept looking for it. I teach Reiki. I guide Sound Baths. But everything I offer comes back to the same thing that saved me: being seen, starting with yourself, so it can finally reach everyone around you too.
