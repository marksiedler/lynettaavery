The Weight Was Never All Yours

You've tried everything. Talk therapy. Western medicine. You followed the protocol, did the work, and still, the anxiety is there. The physical symptom is there. The confusion hasn't lifted.

That's because some of what you're carrying was never yours to begin with.

Family Constellations works with what's hidden beneath the symptom: the entanglements, loyalties, and unspoken burdens passed down through a family system. When what's hidden comes into view, the body recognizes it immediately. And once it's seen, something in you can finally put it down.

The Foundations

How the Work Unfolds

Awareness

Patterns Seeking Resolution

Every symptom, every stuck place, is information. What repeats in a family, an illness, a struggle, a silence, is often a pattern seeking resolution, not a flaw to fix. We look at what's been trying to be seen.

Belonging

Everyone Belongs in the Family

In every family system, everyone belongs, including those who were excluded, forgotten, or never spoken of. When someone is left out of the family soul, the system tries to restore balance, sometimes through someone in a later generation. Naming what was excluded restores order and belonging.

The Body

The Healing Movement

This work is somatic, not intellectual. You don't think your way to insight, you feel into your body, and let the body lead. The movements that arise are often small, unrehearsed, and exactly right.

Lineage

The Mother's Line & The Father's Line

What shows up in your life now often has roots in what moved through the maternal and paternal lines before you: unresolved grief, exclusion, early loss, unacknowledged fate. Seeing where you stand in these two lines is often where clarity begins.

Meet Lynetta

Lynetta Ann Avery

Family Constellations Facilitator  ·  Reiki Teacher  ·  Sound Bath Guide

For a long time, I didn't feel at home in my own skin. Like a tree without roots, I kept reaching for solid ground and never quite finding it.

I looked in a lot of places. Religion. Talk therapy. Twelve-step rooms. Sweat lodge. Each one gave me something real, a piece of the path, a little more light along the way. But underneath it all, the anxiety was still there. Sleep still didn't come easy. I still felt lost, confused, like a river that hadn't found its way to the sea.

What finally brought me home was Family Constellations, Sound Healing, and Reiki. Slowly, what had been tangled began to loosen. What had been hidden came up into the light, root by root, until there was room to breathe. What I found there, steadiness in my body, quiet in my mind, clarity where there had only been confusion, is what I now offer you.
