Family Constellations

Sometimes the pain doesn't belong to you. Sometimes it belongs to your mother, or her mother, or an ancestor whose story was never fully told, and it simply passed down to you, without anyone asking if you wanted it.

Family Constellations is a one-on-one session inspired by Bert Hellinger's work. Using figurines to represent your family system, we let the field show us what's really there: hidden loyalties, inherited burdens, secrets that were never spoken but were always felt. What was carried unconsciously can finally be seen, and released back to where it belongs.

This work is for those who've already done therapy, already done the inner work, and still carry pain, physical or emotional, that won't resolve.

  • One-on-one session using figurines to represent your family system
  • Truth statements spoken with honor and respect
  • In person or on Zoom
