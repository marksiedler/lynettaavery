Remember Who You Are

Do you know where you come from?

Your name. Your homeland. Your parents, your grandparents, and the people before them. Each one carried strengths, challenges and gifts, and each one passed something on to you.

When you know your people, you know your place. You stand a little taller. You are anchored. You belong.

In the story of Moana, her grandmother takes her into a hidden cave and shows her the canoes of her ancestors. Moana learns she comes from voyagers, and everything changes. She can finally say her name, her island and her people, and know who she is.

Later she meets Te Kā, a goddess of fire and rage. Moana walks toward her anyway. She sees what no one else could see: this was Te Fiti, whose heart had been taken. When her heart is returned, she remembers who she is, and the land turns green again.

This is what Family Constellations does.

We look at your family with honor. We find where a heart was taken, where someone was left out, where a burden was passed down. We return what belongs to each person, so you can take your rightful place.

You have a place in your family. You belong. Let's help you remember it.
